Who I Am

More Than Just a Haircut

At KQ Cutz, I do things differently. Every client gets a barber who actually listens, someone who takes the time to understand exactly what you want before a single cut is made. Whether it is a clean fade, a sharp taper, or getting your fringe sitting just right, I make sure you walk out looking exactly how you pictured it.

I work by appointment only, so when you sit in the chair, you have my full attention from start to finish. No rushing, no distractions, just a proper cut done the right way.

Based in the heart of Moate, Co. Westmeath, KQ Cutz is the ultimate spot for teenagers and young men who want a barber who truly gets it.
